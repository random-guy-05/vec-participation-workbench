Review before sharing

Pseudonyms preserve repeated values within the selected column, but they do not make a dataset anonymous. Rare combinations, dates, stage, batch, or other attributes can still identify samples. The alias mapping is discarded when the page is closed and is not included in the export.

Parsing, row sampling, and aliasing happen in this tab. Nothing is uploaded or stored. The browser cannot determine whether a combination of fields is safe to disclose; inspect the preview and follow your institution's policies and the Challenge's current rules.
