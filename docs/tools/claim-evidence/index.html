Claim–Evidence Matrix Builder

Make a research update easier to review: connect each proposed claim to the observation, method, limitation, and next check that support it.

All notes stay in this tab until you choose to copy or download the Markdown. The tool does not assess whether a claim is true.

Trace each proposed claim

Use one card per statement in a report, poster, or discussion. Record enough context that someone can inspect the evidence without overstating what it shows. Up to 20 claims.

Add claims and supporting observations to create a reviewable matrix.



Scope and limitations
  • A claim can be precise and traceable while still being wrong. Ask a subject-matter peer to review both the result and its interpretation.
  • Record whether the evidence is a direct observation, a computed score, a figure, a source, or a hypothesis; do not present them as interchangeable.
  • State uncertainty, alternative explanations, and what would change your interpretation. This builder does not calculate statistical support or validate citations.
  • Review exported text for confidential or identifying material before posting it publicly.
