COMMUNITY COORDINATION · BROWSER-LOCAL

Community Time-Zone Overlap Planner

Find meeting starts that fit a set of local availability windows on a chosen date. Share a timezone and time window—not names, emails, or account details.

The schedule is calculated in this tab and is not uploaded or saved.

Set the date and availability

Enter one participant per line as IANA-time-zone|local-start|local-end. Use a 24-hour clock, for example Europe/Madrid|18:00|21:00. Each line represents one availability window on the selected local date.

Enter the participants' IANA time zones and local windows, then find overlap.

Scope and caveats

  • This searches one chosen calendar date and one non-overnight interval per participant. To schedule across several days, repeat for each date.
  • Candidate starts are checked every 15 minutes. Results use the browser's IANA time-zone database, including its daylight-saving transitions.
  • Local times shown for participants may fall on different UTC dates. Confirm the invitation date and local time with everyone before sending it.
  • This is a candidate finder, not a booking or attendance service. It has no access to calendars or participant identity.

For privacy, omit names and contact details. A timezone and a broad time window are enough to find an overlap.
