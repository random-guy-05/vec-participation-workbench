Median Bootstrap Interval Planner

Estimate a sample median and seeded percentile-bootstrap interval without assuming normally distributed values.

Numeric observations are processed only in this tab. A fixed seed makes the resampling sequence repeatable in this implementation.

Enter one numeric observation per line

Paste at least five observations.

Assumptions and limits

  • The percentile bootstrap resamples observed values independently with replacement; it assumes the observations represent the sampling units of interest.
  • The interval is not BCa, does not model clusters or repeated measures, and can be unstable for small samples or highly discrete data.
  • The seed makes this page's resampling repeatable, not statistically more accurate. More resamples reduce simulation noise but do not fix biased or dependent data.
  • The tool supports 5–1,000 observations and 500–2,000 resamples; it does not remove outliers or impute missing values.
