Use and review
  • A lineage record documents what a team reports; it does not verify that commands ran or that outputs were produced from the claimed inputs.
  • Use stable artifact names, versions, commit IDs, and checksums when safe and available. Keep private paths and access tokens out of the record.
  • Review the final Markdown for data-use restrictions and confidential project details before sharing.
