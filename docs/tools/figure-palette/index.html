Accessibility notes
  • Do not encode meaning with color alone. Add direct labels, shapes, line styles, or patterns when categories matter.
  • Palette swatches on this page do not simulate color-vision deficiencies or measure contrast in your final figure.
  • For ordered values, use a sequential scale; for signed deviations, use a diverging scale with a meaningful center. Avoid rainbow scales.
  • Inspect the final figure at presentation size, in grayscale, and with your community's accessibility guidance.
