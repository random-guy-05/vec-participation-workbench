Paired Median-Difference Bootstrap

Estimate the median within-pair difference and a seeded percentile-bootstrap interval by resampling complete pairs.

Only the derived differences are entered and processed in this tab. The tool does not upload values.

Enter one complete-pair difference per line

Use a consistent direction such as A − B. Do not enter separate A and B rows.

Paste at least five independent pair differences.

Keep the pair as the sampling unit

  • The bootstrap samples complete pair differences with replacement; it preserves within-pair matching.
  • Pairs should be independent across units. Repeated measures nested within people, samples, batches, or embryos need a cluster-aware design.
  • The percentile interval can be unstable for small samples, many ties, or highly discrete differences; it is not BCa and does not correct biased sampling.
  • This estimates a median difference, not a mean difference; state the direction and interval method when reporting it.
