Assumptions and limits

  • This is an approximate Fisher z interval for Pearson correlation, assuming independent paired observations.
  • It does not account for clustering, repeated measures, selection, missing-data mechanisms, or measurement error.
  • At exactly r = −1 or r = 1, the Fisher transform is undefined. For small samples or unusual distributions, use a method justified for your design.
  • A confidence interval does not establish causation, biological importance, or Challenge scoring behavior.
