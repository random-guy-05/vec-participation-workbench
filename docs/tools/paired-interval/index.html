Assumptions and limits

  • The calculation is a two-sided one-sample t interval on paired differences; it is not an independent-groups analysis.
  • Pairs must be valid matches and independent from other pairs. The standard deviation must describe differences, not separate group values.
  • Strongly skewed differences or very small samples may need a design-appropriate method. This calculator does not inspect raw values or missing-pair patterns.
  • The interval does not establish causation, biological importance, or official Challenge scoring behavior.
