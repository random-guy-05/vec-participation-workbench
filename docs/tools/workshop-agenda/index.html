Peer Session Agenda Builder

Create a time-boxed agenda for a peer walkthrough, reading group, or troubleshooting hour—with a hands-on segment and a fallback for people who cannot install software.

The agenda automatically divides time among five segments; adjust it to the group and leave space for questions.

Facilitation note

Keep the session focused on one useful outcome. Offer an observer role and a synthetic-data/no-install route. Do not ask attendees to share private data, credentials, or unpublished results.
