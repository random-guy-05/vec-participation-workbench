Choose the family before interpreting

  • The selected method applies to every submitted row as one family; choosing the family after seeing results changes the question.
  • Holm controls family-wise error under arbitrary dependence; Bonferroni is a simple conservative bound.
  • Benjamini–Hochberg controls FDR under independence and common positive dependence; Benjamini–Yekutieli is more conservative and allows arbitrary dependence.
  • Adjusted values do not validate the model, sampling design, p-value assumptions, or biological interpretation.
