Submission planning · browser-local

VEC Scored Submission Budget Tracker

Keep a local count of scored attempts by phase and scope. Format checks are unlimited and do not spend scored attempts; rejected uploads also do not consume a scored attempt.

Current tracking scope

Choose the phase shown in the current official rules. P1 and P2 limits are per team, task, and UTC day; P3 limits are per team and board for the whole phase. Use a consistent team alias and board label.

Local attempt log

Entries stay in this browser's local storage. Export a backup before clearing browser data. This tracker does not submit files, contact the Challenge, or monitor your account.

Scope and limits

The caps in this tracker reflect Official Rules checked on 14 October 2026: P1 allows 20 scored submissions per team/task/UTC day; P2 allows 8; P3 allows 2 official submissions per board across the phase. The phase selector is manual. Recheck the official rules before relying on a cap because organizers may update them.

Format checks are unlimited. A rejected submission does not use a scored attempt, but uploads have a separate daily limit that this tracker does not count. P3 submissions are scored and published when made and cannot be withdrawn. This is a private planning aid, not an official portal counter.

All records remain in this browser unless you export them. Use a short alias, and avoid credentials or sensitive notes.
