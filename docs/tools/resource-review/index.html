Community Resource Review

Review one public guide or tool from a participant's point of view. Record what works, what blocks the next step, and one change that would help.

Review checks
A useful review includes a reproducible observation and a feasible next step.

Review fairly

Describe what you tried and the conditions you used. Do not treat a single failed setup as proof that a tool is broken; report the exact step and environment. Keep private data, unpublished results, and credentials out of public issue text.
