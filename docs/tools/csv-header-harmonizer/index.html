CSV Header Harmonizer

Apply an explicit, reviewable column-name mapping before stacking or comparing exports from different pipelines.

CSV rows stay in this tab. The tool changes header text only; it does not infer that two columns mean the same thing.

Paste a CSV

Paste a CSV and inspect its header row.

Scope and review

  • Destination names must be non-empty and unique, ignoring case. A mapping that collapses two columns is rejected.
  • The tool does not infer aliases, change cell values, merge columns, or resolve conflicting meanings.
  • The preview shows at most 10 rows. Review the full downloaded CSV and keep the original input.
  • Supports up to 5 MB and 20,000 rows; all processing stays in the browser.
