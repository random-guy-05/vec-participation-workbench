Assumptions and limits

  • This uses the Welch–Satterthwaite degrees of freedom and a two-sided Student t interval for independent groups with unequal variances.
  • The input SDs must be sample standard deviations. The method assumes independent observations within and between groups.
  • It does not account for clustering, paired designs, selection, confounding, or non-random missingness.
  • A confidence interval describes uncertainty in a mean difference; it does not establish causation or biological importance.
