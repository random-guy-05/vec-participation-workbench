How to read the result

The published criterion requires at least two tasks answered with one shared architecture. Task-specific inputs and prediction heads are allowed. Unrelated models per task do not meet the criterion. This checker cannot judge whether two implementations are genuinely the same architecture; the organisers evaluate the method summary.
