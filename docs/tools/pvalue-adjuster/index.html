Definitions and limits

  • Bonferroni controls family-wise error under any dependence but can be conservative.
  • Benjamini–Hochberg controls false discovery rate under stated dependence conditions; Benjamini–Yekutieli is more conservative and valid under arbitrary dependence.
  • The chosen family, test validity, missing hypotheses, and scientific interpretation are your responsibility. Adjusted values do not replace effect sizes or uncertainty intervals.
