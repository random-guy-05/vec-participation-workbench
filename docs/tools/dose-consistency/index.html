Perturbation Dose Consistency Explorer

Compare feature responses across dose levels and independent replicate series using per-replicate rank trends and dose means.

Choose “Load synthetic example” to see the expected input shape, then replace it with a small public or synthetic data extract.

Scope and interpretation

CSV inputs are processed in this browser tab. The page does not upload, store, or fetch pasted data. Rank trends summarize observed monotonic direction; they are not fitted dose-response curves or significance tests.
