A scenario is not a transport guarantee

  • The calculation assumes sensitivity and specificity remain fixed as prevalence changes; this can fail if the population or measurement process changes.
  • Predictive values can move sharply at low prevalence even when sensitivity and specificity are high.
  • Use prevalence scenarios justified by the intended population and record their source; this tool does not estimate prevalence.
  • No uncertainty interval, calibration correction, cost weighting, or threshold optimization is performed.
