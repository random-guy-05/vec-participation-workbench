Groupwise Missingness Auditor

Compare missing-value rates across participant-selected groups to find fields that may need review.

CSV values are processed in this tab. The report contains group/column names and counts, never nonmissing cell values.

Paste a CSV and choose the grouping column

Blank cells are always missing. Tokens are trimmed and matched without case; quoted commas and escaped quotes are supported.

Limits: 5,000 rows, 50 columns, and 20 nonmissing groups.

A pattern is a review signal, not a missingness mechanism

  • A group difference can flag a collection or import issue but does not reveal why values are missing or justify an imputation method.
  • Rows with missing group values are counted separately and excluded from group rates.
  • Review the data dictionary and collection process before modeling; group and field names may reveal sensitive study details.
  • The report is descriptive and does not perform hypothesis tests, imputation, or adjustment for covariates.
