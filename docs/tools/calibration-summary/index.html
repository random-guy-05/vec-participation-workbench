Calibration summaries depend on the sample and bins

  • Brier score is mean squared probability error. It reflects multiple properties of predictions and is not a pure calibration measure.
  • The expected calibration error is a bin-weighted absolute gap and depends on the chosen binning; it can hide within-bin patterns.
  • Use predictions from held-out data and report the bin rule, sample size, and positive prevalence.
  • No uncertainty intervals, recalibration, threshold selection, or cluster adjustment are computed.
