Independent Correlation Difference Comparator

Compare two Pearson correlations from independent samples using Fisher's r-to-z transformation.

Only summary values are needed; no paired records are entered or uploaded.

Enter independent-sample correlations

Enter each correlation and its sample size.

Assumptions and limits

  • This comparison assumes the two samples are independent, the correlations are Pearson correlations, and observations within each sample are independent.
  • It uses the approximate standard error sqrt(1/(n1−3) + 1/(n2−3)) on the Fisher z scale.
  • Do not use this method for overlapping/dependent samples or clustered observations. Use a design-specific comparison.
  • The transformed difference and approximate p-value do not establish causality or biological importance.
