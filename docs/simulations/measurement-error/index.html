Measurement Error Attenuation Explorer

Increase measurement noise in the predictor and compare its fitted slope with the latent-variable slope.

Adjust the controls to explore this deterministic synthetic example.

Assumptions and limits

The generating outcome depends on a latent predictor, while the observed predictor adds independent mean-zero measurement error. Classical predictor error tends to pull the estimated slope toward zero. This is a fixed synthetic example; real measurement error may be differential, correlated with other variables, or otherwise unlike this simple model.

All values are synthetic and generated in this page. The demonstration does not load Challenge data or make a biological finding.
