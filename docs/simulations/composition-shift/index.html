Composition Shift Demonstrator

Change subgroup means and proportions to see why an aggregate comparison can tell a different story.

Composition and within-type change

The aggregate is the weighted mean of the two subgroup means. The reported decomposition splits the aggregate difference symmetrically into composition and within-type components, averaging the two possible replacement orders (a Shapley decomposition).

This is a synthetic two-group demonstration. It does not establish causality, correct for confounding, or replace a model that matches the study design.
