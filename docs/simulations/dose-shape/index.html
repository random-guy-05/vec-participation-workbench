Dose Response Shape Explorer

Compare simple synthetic response shapes and see how the measured dose range affects what can be distinguished.

Range and replication matter

The curves are deterministic teaching examples, not fitted biology. A narrow dose range can make a saturating curve appear linear; sparse doses can miss a peak or threshold. A useful experiment needs vehicle and dose controls, independent biological replicates, and a range justified by feasibility and prior evidence.

Do not infer a mechanism, safe dose, or expected effect from this page. Use it to discuss design choices before analyzing measured data.
