Pseudoreplication Visualizer

See why thousands of cells from a few embryos do not provide thousands of independent biological replicates.

How to read this

The design-effect approximation is 1 + (m − 1) × ICC, where m is cells per replicate. Dividing total cells by this factor gives an illustrative effective cell count under equal cluster sizes. It is not a substitute for biological replicate-level inference, and unequal sample sizes or more complex dependence need other methods.

Use this to build intuition before choosing the biological unit of analysis; do not treat the displayed effective count as a power calculation.
