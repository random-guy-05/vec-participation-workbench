Batch Confounding Playground

Add samples to the missing treatment-by-batch combinations and watch the design matrix regain rank.

The two diagonal combinations each contain 10 illustrative samples.

What rank does and does not tell you

The toy additive design has an intercept plus treatment and batch indicators. With only control/batch 1 and treated/batch 2, treatment is perfectly aliased with batch and the design rank is deficient. Adding both off-diagonal combinations removes exact aliasing.

Full rank is only an identifiability check. It does not guarantee balance, adequate replication, absence of other confounders, or enough power. Counts here stand for independent samples, not cells.
