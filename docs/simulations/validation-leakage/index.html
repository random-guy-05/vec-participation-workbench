Validation Leakage Playground

Estimate how often a random cell split separates a related group across training and validation.

Choose the split unit that matches the question

For a group of m cells assigned independently, the chance the group is split across both partitions is 1 − pm − (1 − p)m, where p is the validation fraction. The calculation assumes independent assignments and a fixed group size.

Holding out embryos, donors, or biological samples tests generalization to new groups. It does not guarantee an unbiased estimate: the groups must still represent the intended deployment setting, and preprocessing must be fit within each training fold.
