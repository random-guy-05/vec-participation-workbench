Regression-to-the-Mean Playground

Select the most extreme baseline observations in a synthetic cohort, then compare their follow-up with the full cohort.

Adjust the controls to explore this deterministic synthetic example.

Assumptions and limits

Every synthetic subject has a stable latent value and independent measurement noise at baseline and follow-up. The follow-up has no treatment or time effect. Selecting observations because their noisy baseline is unusually high creates an expected downward shift on remeasurement; it does not show that treatment caused improvement. The fixed seed makes slider comparisons easier to follow.

All values are synthetic and generated in this page. The demonstration does not load Challenge data or make a biological finding.
